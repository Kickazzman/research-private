How to read this shortlist

The ranking favors regular, photoperiod packs that can plausibly contribute across the eight established mother labels: Original SPG, Sweet Tooth 3.1, Bubble Berry, Blueberry Muffin, Mazar, Cotton Candy Lobster, Zkittlez and Blueberry Grapefruit. Friend-gifted Strawberry Cough and Green Crack—and the possible Granddaddy Purple—remain provisional until grown and verified, so they are not counted in the active breeding plan.

  • Round three checked AK-47 and Lime Gorilla Bubble × GlueChee live on September 23, 2026. Six more candidates carried indexed in-stock language; Mountain Temple, Pura Vida, Wookie Orgasm and X18 remain listing-only.
  • “Quiet” is a breeding judgment, not a guarantee. AK-47, Mountain Temple and Pura Vida have the strongest mother-first case in the new group; Panama, Neville’s Haze, Gorilla Bubble and X18 are deliberately dominant tools.
  • Forum guidance across the surveys converged on selecting males for structure, vigor, stem rub and early trichomes, then progeny-testing before committing. Overgrow also recommends flowering candidate males past pollen drop to identify true resin producers.
  • No candidate-specific hermaphroditism scandal surfaced in the research. Standard practice remains to cull intersex plants before pollen shed.
  • Stock labels are evidence levels: “verified live” means the page was checked in a real browser; “indexed in stock” repeats seller text from the web index; “listing only” needs a cart or email check.
