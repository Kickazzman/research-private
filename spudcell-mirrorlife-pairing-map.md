Dependence is the same class: the designed phages replicate only inside a living host's machinery — no independent replication, exactly the dependence SpudCell has on feeder liposomes, with a different supplier.

### W-07 — Mirror-image proteins (D-proteins and mirror polymerases)
Claim as reported: a 352-residue mirror-image DNA polymerase (D-Dpo4) was chemically synthesised at 9.8% overall yield by one-pot multi-segment condensation (twofold improvement); separately, a mirror-image monobody binder was generated via TRAP display + chemical synthesis, and cross-chiral ligase ribozymes assembled a 129-nt L-RNA.
Sources as reported: D-Dpo4, *Organic & Biomolecular Chemistry* (2025), DOI 10.1039/D5OB00243E; mirror-image monobody, *Nature Communications* 15:10723 (2024), DOI 10.1038/s41467-024-54902-x; cross-chiral L-RNA ligation, *RSC Chemical Biology* (2024), DOI 10.1039/D4CB00253A.
Status (sweep-reported): PEER-REVIEWED (all three).
Relation: CHIRALITY. Same mechanism class as the parts-level work underlying mirror life: inverted-chirality proteins that act only on inverted-chirality substrates. Shared measurement: mirror-enzyme activity on mirror vs natural substrates (specificity, not just synthesis). This is the parts ladder toward a mirror central dogma — polymerases and ribosomal proteins are named as the target components.
Settle/kill: settle by synthesising larger D-proteins (>400 residues, e.g. mirror ribosome components) at usable yield with demonstrated function. Kill if the yield/scale ceiling holds (large D-proteins remain laborious, costly, unscalable) — then parts-level chirality does not imply a path to a mirror cell.
Gap addressed: G1, at parts level only. No mirror-cell measurement follows from parts synthesis.

### W-08 — Anti-mirror countermeasures (enantiomer antibiotics and D-peptide vaccines as proxy experiments)
Claim as reported: enantiomers of four approved antibiotics (ent-chloramphenicol, ent-linezolid, ent-tedizolid, ent-aztreonam) showed minimal activity against natural bacteria (used as a proxy for mirror bacteria), while conjugated D-peptides elicited strong binding antibody responses in adjuvanted mice; the eLife assessment flags the work as incomplete on chiral purity, functional antibody assays, replicates, and pharmacodynamics.
Source as reported: Kleinman et al., eLife reviewed preprint v1, 6 Mar 2026, DOI root 10.7554/eLife.109134.
Status (sweep-reported): PREPRINT (reviewed preprint, with a published assessment noting incompleteness).
Relation: DETECTION / CONTINGENCY. Same contingency logic as the CPL assay: experiment on a PROXY for an organism that does not exist (enantiomer drugs vs natural bacteria standing in for mirror bacteria), before the organism exists. Analogy only on mechanism — this is countermeasure testing, not detection — but the same epistemic structure: a claim about a non-existent system, tested sideways. This candidate is also the source of lead L1 above.
Settle/kill: settle with functional (not just binding) antibody assays, verified chiral purity, and replicated pharmacodynamic readouts. Kill if the antibody response is an adjuvant artifact with no functional activity, or if chiral impurity explains the residual antibiotic activity.
Gap addressed: G1, as a contingency-epistemology parallel, not a chirality measurement.

### W-09 — Homochirality amplification (Kagan non-linear effects and Soai autocatalysis; 2026 Nobel)
Claim as reported: a small enantiomeric imbalance can be amplified to near-purity without outside help: Kagan's non-linear effect (a slightly one-handed catalyst gives a much more one-handed product, 1986) and Soai's asymmetric autocatalysis (the chiral product catalyses its own formation in the same handedness, 1995/2003, to ~99.99% from a trace excess).
Sources as reported: Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences, Nobel Prize in Chemistry 2026 press release, 7 Oct 2026, to Henri B. Kagan and Kenso Soai; underlying: Puchot/Kagan et al., *JACS* 108 (1986); Soai et al., *Nature* 378 (1995).
Status (sweep-reported): PEER-REVIEWED (underlying chemistry, 1986/1995); the 2026 prize is a recognition, not a new origin-of-life result — the Nobel background is explicit that this shows how homochirality CAN emerge, not that it did so on early Earth.
Relation: CHIRALITY. Same mechanism class as any symmetry-breaking account of homochirality: amplification of a small bias. Analogy only to mirror life itself — it explains how one hand could win, not what a mirror cell would do. It is the amplification step that the CPL/contingency work presupposes (a world already one-handed).
Settle/kill: settle for origin-of-life relevance by demonstrating amplification in aqueous, prebiotic-plausible chemistry. Kill that relevance if amplification requires only organozinc/dry-solvent reagents with no early-Earth analogue — the limitation already stated in secondary coverage of the Nobel background.
Gap addressed: G1, as a symmetry-breaking mechanism, not as a mirror-life measurement.

### W-10 — In silico evolution of globular protein folds from random sequences (PFES)
Claim as reported: stable globular protein folds evolve from random amino-acid sequences in atomistic simulation with selection on structure, taking on average 1.15–3 amino-acid replacements per site (some folds at 0.2/site); about half the evolved folds resemble simple natural folds, the rest are unique.
Source as reported: Sahakyan, Babajanyan, Wolf & Koonin, *PNAS* 122(27):e2509015122, 8 July 2025, DOI 10.1073/pnas.2509015122.
Status (sweep-reported): PEER-REVIEWED (simulation study).
Relation: FOLDING + SELECTION. Shared measurement with perg's fold-first framing (S5): fold as the selectable unit, with replacements-per-site as the cost. The fold is what selection acts on, and the "system" here is the simulated population — a fold-first account in silico, with no wet-lab folding, no water/temperature regime, and no system building around the fold demonstrated experimentally. Analogy only to any claim that folding alone bootstraps a living system.
Settle/kill: settle by synthesising PFES-evolved sequences and showing stable globular folds experimentally (not just predicted confidence). Kill if predicted folds fail in vitro or if structure-prediction confidence is doing the selecting rather than physical stability.
Gap addressed: G2, in silico only. S5's water/temperature/system-around legs remain unaddressed experimentally.

### W-12 — Solid-state chiral symmetry breaking with CPL turn-on (Osaka)
Claim as reported: a chiral phenothiazine-derivative crystal undergoes a solid-state transition from an achiral to a chiral crystalline form while maintaining single crystallinity, by inversion of molecular chirality in the lattice with no solvent or impurity input; the transition switches on circularly polarised luminescence.
